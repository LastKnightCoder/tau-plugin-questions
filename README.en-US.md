# Questions

Capture the questions that come up while reading, writing, or chatting with AI, and work through them at your own pace.

## What It Can Do

- **Capture questions**: Jot a question down in the top input bar on the Questions page—include `#group` to file it straight into that group.
- **Organize by group**: Question groups appear in the Sidebar; drag a question onto a group to move it.
- **Link materials**: Attach related cards, articles, project documents, whiteboards, highlights, PDF annotations, web clips, and chats to a question, or create a new article or card directly from the question.
- **Track your trajectory**: Every linked material and status change is logged in the trajectory, and you can add a quick note anytime with **Add a note**.
- **Write your answer**: Write a standalone answer under a question, or turn it into a card, article, or project document that stays in sync wherever you edit it.
- **Go deeper**: Start a chat around a question, or turn it into a Notebook project.

The **Questions in progress** panel on the **Today** page lists questions with recent activity.

## Getting Started

1. Install and enable **Questions** in the Plugin Center.
2. Click **Questions** in the Sidebar and create your first question.

## FAQ

**Do questions sync?** Yes. Questions, groups, answers, and trajectories are stored in your library and sync along with it.

**Is my data kept if I disable the plugin?** Yes. It reappears when you re-enable the plugin; after uninstalling, data is kept for 7 days and restored if you reinstall within that window.
